Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

1Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Kado ("the app", "we") handles when you use the app, and how it is used. Kado is a learning app that turns topics you choose into short practical challenges, with an optional AI coach feature.

2Information we store on your device

Kado is designed to work without requiring an account. The following information is stored locally on your device only, and is not uploaded to any server we operate:

  • The learning paths, topics, goals, and challenge progress you create in the app.
  • Your AI coach chat history, if you have opted in to the AI feature.
  • Your display language preference and AI consent status.

This data stays on your device (or your browser, on the web version) and is removed if you uninstall the app, clear its storage, or clear your browser's site data.

3AI coach feature

The AI coach ("Coach" tab) is optional and requires your explicit consent before use. When enabled, the text of the messages you send is sent to Groq, Inc., a third-party AI provider, in order to generate a reply. We do not control how Groq processes requests on its infrastructure; please avoid sending personal or sensitive information in chat messages.

You can clear your chat history or withdraw your AI consent at any time from Settings → AI & privacy. Withdrawing consent deletes your locally stored chat messages.

4Information we do not collect

Kado does not require you to create an account, does not collect your name, email, or contact details through the app itself, and does not include third-party advertising or analytics tracking.

5Children's privacy

Kado is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

7Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to us — see the Contact Us page.
